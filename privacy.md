# Privacy Policy for Connex Personal Assistant

**Last Updated: November 8, 2026**

Connex Personal Assistant ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application, **Connex Personal Assistant** (the "App").

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage

#### Local-First Data Storage
Connex Personal Assistant is designed as a local-first productivity tool. Your tasks, projects, milestones, calendar entries, schedules, reminders, and custom settings are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit or store your personal productivity data on external cloud servers unless you explicitly configure optional cloud integrations or backup features.

#### Voice Assistant Input
The App includes a local-first Voice Assistant feature. Speech processing and voice recognition are conducted directly on your device where supported by your hardware and operating system. Audio recordings are not stored or transmitted to our servers.

---

### 2. Advertising & Data Collection

Connex Personal Assistant is a free, ad-supported application. To deliver advertisements within the App, we integrate third-party advertising network SDKs, such as Google AdMob.

These third-party ad networks may automatically collect certain device and usage information to serve contextual or personalized advertisements, including:
* **Device Identifiers:** Advertising ID (GAID / AD_ID), IP address, and unique device hardware models.
* **Usage Data:** App interaction, ad view metrics, and diagnostic crash logs.

---

### 3. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies governing data handling:

* **Google Play Services:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* **Google AdMob:** [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

---

### 4. Permissions Used by the App

To provide full productivity functionality, Connex Personal Assistant may request access to the following device permissions:

* **Microphone Access:** Required strictly for speech-to-text processing when using the Voice Assistant.
* **Notifications:** Required to send scheduled reminders for tasks, deadlines, and project milestones.
* **Advertising ID (`com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID`):** Required for displaying third-party advertisements and ad analytics.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion

Since your productivity data is saved locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can clear or delete your tasks, projects, and app data at any time by:
* Deleting individual items directly inside the App.
* Clearing the App's storage via your device settings (**Settings > Apps > Connex > Storage > Clear Data**).
* Uninstalling the App from your device.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

Connex Personal Assistant is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.
